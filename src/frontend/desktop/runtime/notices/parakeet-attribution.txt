INT8 quantization, and a rebuilt preprocessor. Animama redistributes a subset;
no FP16 or FP32 model is included. Preserve this attribution, the CC BY 4.0
reference, the source ATTRIBUTION.md and the identified changes with model
source archives and optional packs. No endorsement by NVIDIA is implied.
The producer's config/vocab manifest hashes are stale; direct files at the
pinned revision match the local bytes and are the applicable source receipts.
