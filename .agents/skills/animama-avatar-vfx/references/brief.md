# Brief and interview

Every effect starts as a short written brief. It is what the art, the
settings, any Reaction assignment and the owner's review are judged against.

## Interview

Ask only what would change the effect, a few questions at most, each with
your recommended answer first. Typical ones:

- **What it expresses and when:** "Nervous sweat when the Anima is caught
  out — for nervousness and embarrassment Reactions?"
- **How loud:** a small accent (intensity 1–2) or a big comic beat (3)?
- **Where:** beside the head (sticker), on the face, over the eyes, on the
  body, or the whole silhouette?
- **How long and how it moves:** a quick pop (under 1 s), held while
  talking, or looping?

Skip the interview when the request already answers these or the owner says
to use your judgement; state the choices you made in the brief.

## Brief template

```markdown
### <Name> (`<stem>`)

- **Expresses:** <emotion or situation, one line>
- **Beat:** <the comedic or emotional moment it lands; comedy preferred>
- **Anime reference:** <the trope it borrows and a well-known example, e.g.
  "Frieren's deadpan bar eyes", "the anger vein", "> < flustered eyes">
- **Attachment:** <face_landmark eyes | face_surface blush | sticker around
  head | body chest | silhouette tint …> — <placement detail>
- **Reactions:** <emotions and intensity tiers 1–3 it suits, or "preview only">
- **Motion and duration:** <e.g. pops in with a 0.15 s scale overshoot, bobs
  gently, held for the Reaction; flipbook 8 frames at 10 fps>
- **Style:** <Ink (manga G-pen ink and screentone) | Marker (crayon and
  marker doodle)> — <why it suits the gag>
- **Art:** <what is drawn, colours; single image, boil or frame-by-frame
  flipbook; other-eye image?>
- **Budget:** <effects:assets budget name; it follows the attachment
  (floating face art `landmark`, painted `surface`, `body`, `sticker`,
  `silhouette`, flipbook sheets `flipbook`); say why if larger>
- **Face:** <eyes hidden or kept; mouth cleanup> (face effects only)
```

## Good briefs

- One clear idea per effect. "Sweat drop on the temple" reads instantly;
  "sweat plus blush plus lines" is three effects that can coexist.
- Comedy comes from exaggeration and timing: a big drop that slides, a
  steam puff on every beat, shade that falls like a curtain.
- Match intensity: tier 1 effects are subtle accents or none; tier 3 can be
  loud. Not every Reaction needs an effect — add one only where it lands a
  beat.
- Mouth effects sit on the lip-synced mouth: brief them as preview-only.
- Name stems in lowercase with `_` (`sweat_drop`), matching Included effects.
