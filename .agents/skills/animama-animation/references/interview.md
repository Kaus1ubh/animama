# Clarifying interview

A motion prompt leaves most of the performance open: "a shy wave" can be a
small wrist flick or a whole-arm wave behind a raised shoulder. Before
planning, ask the owner about the choices that would change the clip, and
record the answers as the brief you author from.

## When to ask

- Ask when an answer would change the beats, the body regions involved, a
  contact or the face. Skip what you can decide from the prompt, the existing
  Library or these references.
- Skip the interview when the prompt already settles the performance, when
  the owner says to go ahead with your judgement, or for a revision whose
  notes already say what to change.
- Ask once, before writing the clip. Ask at most four questions at a time,
  each with two to four concrete options and your recommendation first. Use
  the structured question tool when the host has one.

## What to ask about

Pick the questions that matter for this prompt:

| Topic | Example options |
| --- | --- |
| When chat uses it | greeting; reaction to a joke; while explaining |
| One-shot or held | plays once and settles; holds and loops while the Anima keeps the state |
| Intensity | subtle (head and face); medium (one arm); big (whole body) |
| Personality | shy; cheeky; formal; energetic |
| Leading hand | right; left; both |
| Contacts | which hand touches what (cheek, chin, chest, hip, the other hand) |
| Face | its own expression (which one); keep the Anima's current emotion |
| Length | about 1.5 s; 2–3 s; a long 10–20 s sequence |
| Reference | a known gesture, meme or character pose to match |
| Name and Selector ID | the Library name; the snake_case ID chat selects it by |

## Record the brief

Write the answers as a short brief: name, Selector ID, one-sentence intent,
one-shot or held, length, intensity, leading hand, contacts, face, and the
planned beats with times. Show it to the owner with your defaults filled in
for anything not asked, and author from it once the owner confirms. Keep the
brief in the hand-off so later revisions can see the intent.

For a batch, ask about every item in one round and show one table of briefs
to confirm (see [batch mode](batch.md)).
